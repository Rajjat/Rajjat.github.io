About

Researcher with a PhD in Artificial Intelligence and 8 years of experience building machine learning systems.

I am a Senior Research Scientist and Project Lead at the University of Bonn. My work spans large language models, multimodal learning, reinforcement learning, graph neural networks, and geospatial representation learning.

I am particularly interested in turning research ideas into systems that work in practice: evaluation frameworks that expose model failure modes, scalable ML pipelines, and intelligent systems for mobility and spatial reasoning.
